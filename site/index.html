About

Installer is The Verge’s weekly newsletter by David Pierce that delivers recommendations for apps, games, media, and gadgets. This site indexes every recommendation from every issue to make them all easier to find.

You can search by names, text descriptions, and tags. You can also filter by category, the newsletter section the item appeared in, tags, or the year. Every entry links to the thing itself and the issue it came from. This site does not have any affiliate links placed by me and is not monetized.

I’m just a big fan of the newsletter and I created this for personal use. It is unofficial and completely unaffiliated with The Verge. The short excerpts are quoted with attribution and all newsletter content belongs to The Verge and Vox Media. If you spot a problem or have feedback, you can open an issue on GitHub or contact me directly. Also feel free to contribute to the open-source project if you want to help make it better.
