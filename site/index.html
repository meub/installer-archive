About

Installer is The Verge’s weekly newsletter by David Pierce — apps, games, shows, gadgets, and reader finds, every Saturday. This site indexes every recommendation from every issue so you can actually find them again.

Search matches names, blurbs, and tags. Filter by category, the newsletter section an item appeared in, tags, or year. Every entry links to the thing itself and to the issue it came from.

Unofficial and unaffiliated. Short excerpts are quoted with attribution; all newsletter content belongs to The Verge / Vox Media. Something here that shouldn’t be? Email alexmeub@gmail.com.
